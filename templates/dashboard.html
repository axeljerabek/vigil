Automate with a cronjob instead of clicking the buttons

Add to your crontab (crontab -e) — generates today's summary shortly after midnight, and a weekly one every Monday, both waiting in this card next time you open the dashboard: 

5 0 * * *  cd /path/to/vigil && ./.venv/bin/python daily_summary.py --period day
10 0 * * 1 cd /path/to/vigil && ./.venv/bin/python daily_summary.py --period week

Replace /path/to/vigil with your actual install path. Uses the same virtual environment and Ollama settings as the rest of the pipeline — nothing extra to configure.
